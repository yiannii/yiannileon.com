Hello! My name is Yianni Leontakianakos. I am an Information Technology student at Western Governors University. I study remotely and live on Long Island, New York.

The purpose of this website is to give myself a space to exercise skills in web development, system administration, and networking by designing, building and hosting a website by myself. In keeping with that spirit, this site is built from the ground up, without using a foundation of ai-generated code or "vibe coding". In addition to that, I hope to use this site as a personal blog where I can post about my current projects.

Outside of technology, in my spare time, I enjoy fitness, music production, spending time outdoors and learning about subjects such as history, art, anthropology, philosophy and theology.

I am currently looking for work!
